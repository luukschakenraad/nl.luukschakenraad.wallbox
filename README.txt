Unofficial community app for Wallbox EV chargers, not affiliated with Wallbox. Rebuilt from Wallbox Charger by Roy K; if you used that app, pair your charger again in this one and remove the old app.

Start and stop charging from any flow, set the charging current, use solar charging (Eco-Smart), and see what the charger is doing: a detailed status (scheduled, car full, waiting for solar or power, locked with car, …), whether a car is connected, the current that flows next to the set current, the Eco-Smart mode, and a connection alarm. Flows hear when the car connects or leaves, when a session ends, and when the charger didn't follow a start or stop. Works with Homey Energy: set target power mode to Homey and the current follows the target for the phases your car charges on; a lifetime meter shows all energy charged. Built to stay within the Wallbox cloud limits, with an adjustable polling interval.

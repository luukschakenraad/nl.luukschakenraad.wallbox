An unofficial community app for Wallbox chargers, not affiliated with Wallbox. If you used Wallbox Charger before, pair your charger again in this app and then remove the old one.

See what your charger is doing and why, and let your flows start, stop and steer charging, also on solar power with Eco-Smart. With Homey Energy, the charger can follow a target power. The app stays within the limits of the Wallbox cloud.
